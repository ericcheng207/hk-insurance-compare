What are Virtual Insurers?

Virtual insurers in Hong Kong are fully digital insurance companies that operate without traditional agents. They offer transparent pricing, instant quotes, and streamlined online claims — all regulated by the Insurance Authority.

💰 No Commissions

Virtual insurers cut out middlemen and agent commissions, passing savings directly to you with more competitive premiums.

⚡ Instant Quotes

Get quotes in minutes, not days. Compare plans and prices instantly without waiting for agent callbacks.

📱 Digital Claims

Submit claims via mobile app with AI-powered processing for faster approvals and reimbursements.

🔍 Full Transparency

Clear pricing, policy terms, and coverage details displayed upfront — no hidden fees or surprises.
